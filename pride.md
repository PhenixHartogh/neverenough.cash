# 🌈 Pride

Welcome, and thank you for visiting.

Pride is celebrating the diversity, resilience, and achievements of LGBTQIA+ communities while recognising the importance of inclusion, respect, and equality.

We believe everyone deserves to feel safe, valued, and empowered to be themselves.

## Our Commitment

We are committed to creating an inclusive environment where everyone can:

* Be their authentic selves.
* Feel respected and welcomed.
* Experience a workplace and community free from discrimination.
* Learn from diverse perspectives and lived experiences.

Inclusion is an ongoing journey, and we're committed to continually learning and improving.

## Support Services (Australia)

If you, a friend, family member, or colleague would like support, these services are available:

### 🏳️‍🌈 QLife (Australia-wide)

Free, anonymous peer support for LGBTQIA+ people.

* Phone: **1800 184 527**
* Webchat: https://qlife.org.au

### 🌈 Rainbow Door (Victoria)

Specialist support, information, and referrals for LGBTQIA+ Victorians, their families and supporters.

* Phone: **1800 729 367**
* Text: **0480 017 246**
* https://rainbowdoor.org.au

### 💚 Switchboard Victoria

Community-led support, resources, and referral services.

https://www.switchboard.org.au

### 🧡 Lifeline Australia

24-hour crisis support for anyone experiencing emotional distress.

* Phone: **13 11 14**
* https://www.lifeline.org.au

### 🧠 Medicare Mental Health

Free mental health support and referrals.

* Phone: **1800 595 212**
* https://www.medicarementalhealth.gov.au
